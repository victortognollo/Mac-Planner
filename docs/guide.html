Installation

4

Allow it in Privacy & Security

Open System Settings (Apple menu › System Settings) and go to Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to Planner.

General Appearance Notifications Privacy & Security Desktop & Dock Internet Accounts
Security
“Planner” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway

Confirm with your password or Touch ID. If another warning appears, click Open Anyway again. You only do this once.

Shortcut for Terminal users (optional)

Instead of step 4, open the Terminal app, paste the line below and press Return. Then open Planner as usual.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Planner.app
5

Allow notifications

Planner alerts you at reminder times, 5 minutes before meetings and when the focus timer ends. When it asks, click Allow.

Move and resize

Drag the top strip, where the month is. Pull the edges to resize: below the normal size, everything shrinks together.

Add

Click the + on any day: a task (crossed out when done), a meeting with a link, or a reminder with a time.

Edit and move

Double-click the text to edit. Drag the text to another day. Right-click for more options.

Focus timer

Pick 15, 25, 45, 60 or type any minutes. When it ends, a sound plays and the time is added to the day.

Language, layout and text

In the ⋯ button: English or Portuguese, horizontal (days in columns) or vertical (days in rows) layout, and text size.

Position on screen

In the ⋯ button: on the desktop behind windows (default), normal window, or always on top.

Optional

Sync your calendars

Planner shows the events from your Mac's Calendar next to your tasks and keeps them up to date.

  1. Click the calendar button at the top of Planner and turn on Sync with your Mac calendars.
  2. When macOS asks, click Allow Full Access.
  3. Google, Outlook or iCloud: in the same button, choose “Google, Outlook or iCloud account…”, sign in and turn on Calendars.
  4. Any other website: paste the calendar’s .ics link and click Subscribe.

Common questions

Planner disappeared from the screen

It sits behind your windows. Show the desktop (F11, or spread your fingers on the trackpad) or choose ⋯ › Position on screen › Always on top.

I don’t get alerts

System Settings › Notifications › Planner: turn on “Allow notifications”.

My calendars don’t show up

System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars: turn on Planner.

It doesn’t open when my Mac starts

In Planner, ⋯ › Open at login. Also check System Settings › General › Login Items.

Where are my tasks stored?

Only on this Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Planner. Each Mac has its own.
